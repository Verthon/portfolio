# CONTEXT

Personal technical blog at https://sordyl.dev. Author: Krzysztof Sordyl.

Niche: frontend architecture, developer experience, technical decisions with business impact.
Voice: direct, experience-backed, no filler. Titles are concrete, not clickbait.

## The three content types

**Blog post** — long-form. A war story, a migration, or an architecture decision. Featured posts appear on the home page.

**Dev bite** — short how-to. One problem, one solution. Body follows a fixed shape: Context, Problem, Solution.

**Observatory** — a tracking note about a web tool that is not yet stable. Records status, trade-offs, and adoption risk. Not a tutorial. Each note states a **Status** and a **Recommendation**.
